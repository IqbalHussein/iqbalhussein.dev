about

I'm a developer who likes problems where the interesting part is hidden: the shape of a football attack, the load of a training week, the chain of requests behind one click.

Most of what I build tries to make that hidden part visible and checkable. PitchScanner puts a confidence interval on every number. Apricity lists every adjustment its agent makes and why. Room Booker reports exactly which step failed and what it saw. I'd rather a tool admit what it doesn't know than look certain.

I work across the stack, from data pipelines and search to interfaces, and I like writing the benchmark before trusting the result.
