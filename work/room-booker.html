Room Booker

Study rooms at Carleton's library fill up fast. This service runs every night in Docker and books the same room seven days ahead. It reads slot times from the booking system's own availability data instead of guessing from the page, and refuses to click if the two disagree.

What it does

  • Every run ends as succeeded, skipped, unavailable or failed. A room that's already taken isn't a failure.
  • Failures name the step that broke and what the booker actually saw.
  • Slot matching and parsing are unit-tested against recorded responses, without a browser.
