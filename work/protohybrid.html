ProtoHybrid

Lifting apps and running apps don't know about each other. ProtoHybrid puts runs and lifts on one weekly plan, shows each day's load, and flags the clashes you set rules for, such as heavy legs within 24 hours of a hard run.

Open protohybrid.vercel.appSource on GitHub
Built with
Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Postgres
Status
Live

What it does

  • Drag sessions to reschedule, and compare what was planned with what happened.
  • Log sets against an exercise library, with routines, a rest timer and personal records.
  • Pulls busy time from Google Calendar so training fits around everything else.
  • Sign in with a one-time email link.
