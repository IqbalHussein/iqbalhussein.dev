PitchScanner

Place the ball, the attackers and the defenders on a pitch, and PitchScanner searches StatsBomb's open 360 data, about 1.3 million frames from 426 matches, for the closest real situations. Then it shows what those moments led to: how often they produced a shot, how much xG they were worth, how often possession was lost.

Open pitchscanner.vercel.appSource on GitHub
Built with
Python, FastAPI, Postgres with pgvector, React, TypeScript
Status
Live

What it does

  • Every outcome comes with a confidence interval and the baseline for that part of the pitch, so a claim that a shape is dangerous can be checked.
  • Filters are written in plain language, like “Bayern at home, last fifteen minutes, trailing”. Rules parse them first; a model is only a fallback.
  • Situations are encoded three ways (grid, polar and tactical) and searched with pgvector.
  • The query path is built for a 150 ms p95.
